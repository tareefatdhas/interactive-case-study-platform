# Classfully product copy

## One line

Classfully is the interactive layer for university courses.

## Short description

Classfully helps instructors run live polls, pulse checks, quizzes, questions, and classroom activities while students build a private record of participation and progress across the course.

## Standard description

Classfully makes university classrooms interactive without asking instructors to move their slides or lesson materials. Instructors can prepare activities for each class session, launch them when the moment is right, and see questions, sentiment, understanding, and participation as responses arrive. Students join from their phones, contribute without interrupting the room, and build a private record of progress, points, and course activity over time.

## Positioning line

Make the classroom interactive. Build every student's journey.

## Product facts

- Built for university classrooms and seminar-sized teaching moments
- Works alongside the instructor's existing slides
- Student-first mobile participation
- Live presentation and instructor control views
- Session plans with polls, pulse checks, quizzes, word clouds, group work, peer learning, timers, and classroom tools
- Persistent participation and progress records
- Student questions and upvoting without interrupting the instructor

## Preferred language

Use classroom, class, session, activity, response, participation, progress, and instructor.

Avoid engagement platform, learning ecosystem, unlock potential, seamless experience, revolutionize, and other generic product jargon.
